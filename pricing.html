Important Note

Why Prices Can Vary

Pricing depends on vehicle size, condition, pet hair, stains, salt buildup, spills, odours, and how much time is needed to safely complete the detail.

Extremely neglected vehicles may require additional time or add-on pricing.

Understanding Detailing Value

Why Professional Detailing Takes Time

A professional detail is far more than a quick wash and vacuum. Depending on vehicle size and condition, a proper detail can take anywhere from 3 to 8+ hours.

Time is spent vacuuming, cleaning interior panels, treating carpets and seats, cleaning cracks and crevices, washing exterior surfaces, cleaning wheels, cleaning glass, and completing a final inspection.

Pet hair, stains, salt buildup, spills, odours, and heavily used family vehicles often require additional time and attention to restore properly.

Our pricing reflects the time, care, products, equipment, training, and attention required to deliver professional results — not a rushed surface clean.
