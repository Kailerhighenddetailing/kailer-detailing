Kailer’s Story

Trust Is Earned. Every Detail Matters.

Kailer’s High End Detailing was built on patience, honesty, professional training, and real pride in the work. This is the story behind the business.

It is not just about cleaning vehicles. It is about building confidence, earning trust, improving every year, and giving customers a detailing experience that feels personal, respectful, and professional.

Kailer’s High End Detailing

Built on trust, pride, patience, and care.

The Mission

Built for the long term — not just the next appointment.

Kailer’s High End Detailing was built on a simple belief: trust is earned, and it is earned through every detail.

My name is Kailer. I started this business because I genuinely enjoy working on vehicles and seeing the transformation that comes from careful, professional detailing.

Detailing gives me a way to use patience, routine, focus, and attention to detail in a meaningful way. Every vehicle is a chance to improve, build confidence, and create a result the customer can feel proud of.

As the business grows, my goal is to keep learning, keep improving, and keep building long-term trust with customers who want their vehicle handled with care.

Who Is Kailer?

A detailer building a future through work, training, and trust.

Kailer is 23 years old and built this business around honesty, craftsmanship, continued education, and a real passion for helping customers protect and maintain their vehicles.

Kailer has autism and verbal apraxia, which means communication and speech can sometimes take more time. He believes in being honest and transparent about that, because customers deserve to understand the person behind the business.

While he may communicate differently, his goal never changes: take the time to understand the customer, respect the vehicle, and complete the work with care.

“I may communicate differently, but I take my time to make sure every customer is understood and every vehicle receives the care it deserves.”

The Journey

From hands-on experience to building a professional business.

Every step helped build the skill, confidence, patience, and pride behind Kailer’s High End Detailing.

Step 1

Leung’s Auto

Kailer started building hands-on experience around vehicles while helping at his uncle’s shop. This helped him become more comfortable with vehicles, tools, work routines, and customer expectations.

Step 2

Detailing Experience

He continued developing his detailing skills through additional hands-on experience, learning the value of consistency, patience, careful cleaning, and quality control.

Step 3

Owner’s Pride Training

Kailer trained with Owner’s Pride Detailing School in St. Albert, strengthening his professional process, product knowledge, and attention to quality.

Step 4

Professional Standards

Because detailing is largely unregulated, Kailer chose to take training, education, licensing, insurance, and professional presentation seriously.

Future

Continued Growth

Future goals include more training, better equipment, expanded services, community involvement, and becoming one of Edmonton’s most trusted detailing businesses.
