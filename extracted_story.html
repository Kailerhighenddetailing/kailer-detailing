Our Mission

Built for the long term — not just the next appointment.

Kailer’s High End Detailing was built on a simple belief: trust is earned, and we earn it with every detail.

Who Is Kailer?

A detailer building a future through hard work, training, and trust.

Kailer built this business on honesty, craftsmanship, continued education, and a genuine passion for helping customers protect and maintain their vehicles.

While he may communicate differently due to verbal apraxia, his focus never changes: taking the time to ensure every customer is understood and every vehicle receives the care, attention, and professionalism it deserves.

At Kailer’s High End Detailing, trust isn't given — it's earned with every detail.

My name is Kailer, I am 23 years old, and I started Kailer’s High End Detailing because I genuinely love working on vehicles and seeing the transformation that comes from professional detailing.

I have autism and verbal apraxia, which means communication and speech can sometimes be difficult for me. I believe in being honest and transparent with my customers.

When people meet me, they may notice that I communicate differently, and I never want that to come across the wrong way. I simply process and communicate in my own way.

The Journey

More Than A Business

Long before Kailer's High End Detailing existed, Kailer spent years learning around vehicles, helping at his uncle's shop, developing work habits, and building confidence through hands-on experience.

Like many people with autism and verbal apraxia, some things have come with additional challenges. Communication can take more time, but patience, attention to detail, consistency, and dedication have always been strengths.

Detailing became a way to combine those strengths into something meaningful. Every vehicle is an opportunity to improve, learn, build trust, and create something customers can be proud of.

What started as a passion has grown into a business built around professionalism, education, customer care, and long-term growth.

Kailer’s Journey

From learning the trade to building a professional business

Every step helped build the skill, patience, confidence, and pride behind Kailer’s High End Detailing.

Step 1

Leung’s Auto

Kailer started building hands-on experience around vehicles while working with his uncle at Leung’s Auto. This helped develop his comfort around vehicles, tools, work routines, and customer expectations.

Step 2

West Edmonton Detailing Experience

He continued building his detailing skills through additional experience in West Edmonton, learning the importance of consistency, patience, and careful cleaning.

Step 3

Owner’s Pride Training

Kailer trained with Owner’s Pride Detailing School in St. Albert, strengthening his professional detailing process, product knowledge, and attention to quality.

Step 4

International Detailing Association Member

Because the detailing industry is largely unregulated, Kailer joined the IDA to show customers that professionalism, education, and standards matter.

Step 5

Business Launch

Kailer’s High End Detailing was created to offer Edmonton customers honest service, careful work, fair pricing, and a more personal detailing experience.

Future

Continued Training & Expanded Services

This is only the beginning. Future goals include advanced detailing education, additional certifications, improved equipment, expanded service offerings, community involvement, and becoming one of Edmonton's most trusted detailing businesses.
