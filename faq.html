Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before booking with Kailer's High End Detailing. Clear answers about pricing, timing, services, guarantees, and appointments.

How long does a detail take?

Most appointments take between 3–8 hours depending on package, vehicle size, and condition.

Do I need an appointment?

Yes. All services are appointment-only so every vehicle receives proper time and attention.

Do you offer interior-only details?

Yes. Interior-only, exterior-only, and full detailing packages are available.

What if my vehicle is extremely dirty?

That is okay. Heavy pet hair, salt, stains, spills, mud, or odours may require extra time or add-on pricing.

Do you remove pet hair?

Yes. Pet hair removal is available and may require additional time depending on severity.

Do you remove stains?

Many stains can be improved or removed, but results depend on the stain type, age, and material.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Kailer's High End Detailing is licensed and insured for customer peace of mind.

What training do you have?

Kailer completed professional training through Owner's Pride and is a proud IDA member.

Do you guarantee your work?

Yes. If something was missed, contact us within 24 hours and we will make it right.

How do I get a quote?

You can use the quote form, call, text, or message online. Photos help provide the most accurate estimate.

Why are your prices different from some other detailers?

Every detail includes professional equipment, premium products, licensing, insurance, continued training, and the time required to achieve professional results. Our focus is delivering exceptional workmanship and long-term value rather than rushing vehicles through to maximize volume.
