Customer Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to common questions about booking, timing, vehicle condition, pet hair, interiors, exteriors, and what to expect before your detail.

How long does a detail take?

Time depends on vehicle size, condition, and the level of service being completed. Heavy dirt, salt, stains, spills, or pet hair can require additional time.

My vehicle is extremely dirty. Is that okay?

Yes. Every vehicle is different, and there is no judgment. The focus is simply helping restore, refresh, and improve the condition of your vehicle.

Do you remove pet hair?

Yes. Pet hair removal is available as an add-on service. Excessive pet hair may require additional time depending on the amount and how embedded it is.

How do I book?

You can book online, call or text, message through social media, or submit a quote request form. If you are unsure which service is right, a quote request is usually the best place to start.

Do I need an appointment?

Yes. All services are by appointment only so every vehicle receives proper time, attention, and care.

Do you clean interiors and exteriors?

Yes. Kailer's High End Detailing offers interior detailing, exterior detailing, and full vehicle detailing depending on your vehicle’s needs.

Can you remove stains and odours?

Many stains and odours can be improved, but results depend on the source, age, material, and severity. Some stains or odours may not fully disappear, but the goal is always to improve the vehicle safely and honestly.

Is shampooing included?

Shampooing depends on the service selected. If your seats, carpets, or mats need deeper cleaning, mention that when requesting a quote so the right service can be recommended.

What should I remove before my appointment?

Please remove personal belongings, valuables, car seats, important documents, and anything you do not want handled before your appointment.

Do you service large SUVs, trucks, or family vehicles?

Yes. Larger vehicles are welcome, but they may require more time because there is more surface area, more seats, more carpet, and often more storage areas to clean.

Still Unsure?

Have a question that is not listed?

Reach out anytime. The goal is to make the process clear before you book, so you feel confident about the service your vehicle needs.
