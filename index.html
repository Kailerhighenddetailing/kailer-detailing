Why Detailing Times Vary

Many customers are surprised to learn that professional detailing often takes several hours to complete. Unlike a quick wash and vacuum, detailing involves careful cleaning, inspection, and attention to hundreds of interior and exterior surfaces.

The time required depends on vehicle size, condition, pet hair, stains, salt buildup, and the level of cleaning or restoration needed.

  • Executive Detail: Approx. 3–4 hours
  • Signature Detail: Approx. 5–6 hours
  • Ultimate Detail: Approx. 6–8+ hours

These time estimates are consistent with the level of work commonly required for professional detailing services and may vary depending on vehicle condition.

We encourage customers to research detailing services, compare packages, ask questions, and choose the option that best fits their vehicle, expectations, and budget.

Why Detailing Matters

More than just a clean vehicle

Professional detailing helps improve comfort, maintain your vehicle, and create a cleaner driving experience.

Protects Your Interior

Dirt, salt, spills, and debris can slowly wear down carpets, plastics, seats, and interior surfaces over time.

Improves Comfort

A clean vehicle creates a more enjoyable and comfortable driving experience for both daily use and passengers.

Maintains Vehicle Value

Keeping a vehicle clean and maintained can help preserve appearance and resale value over time.

Removes Salt & Debris

Edmonton roads, winter salt, mud, and buildup can collect quickly and become difficult to manage without proper cleaning.

Healthier Environment

Deep interior cleaning can help reduce dust, allergens, odors, and bacteria trapped inside the vehicle.

FAQ

Common Questions

How long does a detail take?

Time depends on vehicle size and condition. Heavy dirt, salt, stains, or pet hair may take longer.

My vehicle is extremely dirty. Is that okay?

Absolutely. Every vehicle is different, and there is never any judgment. The focus is simply helping restore and improve the condition of your vehicle.

Do you remove pet hair?

Yes. Pet hair removal is available as a +$50 add-on. Excessive pet hair may require additional time.

How do I book?

You can book online through our Square booking system, call, text, message us on Facebook, or submit a free quote request form.

Do I need an appointment?

Yes. All services are by appointment to ensure every vehicle receives the time and attention it deserves.

Do you clean interiors and exteriors?

Yes, Kailer’s High End Detailing offers both interior and exterior detailing services.
