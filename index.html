FAQ

Common Questions

How long does a detail take?

Time depends on vehicle size and condition. Heavy dirt, salt, stains, or pet hair may take longer.

My vehicle is extremely dirty. Is that okay?

Absolutely. Every vehicle is different, and there is never any judgment. The focus is simply helping restore and improve the condition of your vehicle.

Do you remove pet hair?

Yes. Pet hair removal is available as a +$50 add-on. Excessive pet hair may require additional time.

How do I book?

You can book online through our Square booking system, call, text, message us on Facebook, or submit a free quote request form.

Do I need an appointment?

Yes. All services are by appointment to ensure every vehicle receives the time and attention it deserves.

Do you clean interiors and exteriors?

Yes, Kailer’s High End Detailing offers both interior and exterior detailing services.
