Why Detailing Matters

More than just a clean vehicle

Professional detailing helps improve comfort, maintain your vehicle, and create a cleaner driving experience.

Protects Your Interior

Dirt, salt, spills, and debris can slowly wear down carpets, plastics, seats, and interior surfaces over time.

Improves Comfort

A clean vehicle creates a more enjoyable and comfortable driving experience for both daily use and passengers.

Maintains Vehicle Value

Keeping a vehicle clean and maintained can help preserve appearance and resale value over time.

Removes Salt & Debris

Edmonton roads, winter salt, mud, and buildup can collect quickly and become difficult to manage without proper cleaning.

Healthier Environment

Deep interior cleaning can help reduce dust, allergens, odors, and bacteria trapped inside the vehicle.

About Me

My Story

My name is Kailer, I am 23 years old, and I started Kailer’s High End Detailing because I genuinely love working on vehicles and seeing the transformation that comes from professional detailing.

I have autism and verbal apraxia, which means communication and speech can sometimes be difficult for me. I believe in being honest and transparent with my customers. When people meet me, they may notice that I communicate differently, and I never want that to come across the wrong way. I simply process and communicate in my own way.

Detailing became something I could fully focus on and take pride in because I genuinely enjoy transforming vehicles and helping customers feel proud of their car again. I also value building strong relationships, creating a trusted network, and being able to grow a business while staying true to who I am.

I worked with my uncle at Leung’s Auto, continued building my detailing skills in West Edmonton, and trained with Owner’s Pride Detailing School in St. Albert. Because the detailing industry is largely unregulated, I also joined the IDA so customers know I am serious about professionalism, continued learning, and industry standards.

I built this business to prove that dedication, attention to detail, professionalism, and honesty matter more than fitting into a stereotype.

“I may communicate differently due to verbal apraxia, but I take my time to make sure every customer is understood and every vehicle receives the attention and care it deserves.”

This is only the beginning. I am committed to continuing my training, expanding my detailing knowledge, and investing into better skills, tools, and techniques over time. Follow along as Kailer’s High End Detailing continues to grow.

How long does a detail take?

Absolutely. Every vehicle is different, and there is never any judgment. The focus is simply helping restore and improve the condition of your vehicle.

Do you remove pet hair?

Yes, pet hair removal can be included depending on the condition of the vehicle.

Do you clean interiors and exteriors?

Yes, Kailer’s High End Detailing offers both interior and exterior detailing services.
